# ResourceX — Visual Direction

## Three possible directions

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signal Atlas | A dark cartographic instrument panel where routes, resource states, and disruption signals behave like a living operational atlas. It feels calm, intelligent, and consequential rather than decorative. | 0.07 |
| Industrial Monograph | A restrained technical publication aesthetic, using deep ink, warm mineral tones, and oversized data typography to make procurement feel rigorous and archival. | 0.04 |
| Blackwater Relay | A lower-light spatial system inspired by marine navigation rooms, with selective cyan signals cutting through graphite machinery and near-black space. | 0.09 |

## Selected approach — Signal Atlas

### Design Movement

**Signal Atlas** blends scientific cartography, industrial instrumentation, and understated cinematic interface design. The product opens as a responsive world model, then contracts into crisp purchasing and logistics surfaces without losing its sense of place.

### Core Principles

1. **Spatial information earns its depth.** Routes, ports, factories, and disruptions are shown in layered geography only when spatial relation materially improves a decision.
2. **Signals, not decoration.** Cyan, green, amber, and red are reserved for intelligence states, verification, and risk — never for ambient spectacle alone.
3. **Cinematic to operational.** The overview has atmosphere and motion; procurement surfaces are stable, quiet, and scan-friendly.
4. **Trust through structure.** Each AI conclusion is paired with evidence, recency, confidence, and a practical next action.

### Color Philosophy

The workspace is built from **coal-black, graphite, and midnight slate** so active routes feel physically luminous without turning the whole interface neon. **Signal Cyan (#38D6EA)** is the ownable intelligence color: it identifies live flows and focused states. **Viridian (#4CD9A5)** verifies health, **amber** interrupts attention for watch states, and **coral red** is preserved for actual disruption. A soft mineral white makes data readable, while an almost-black navy separates active terrain from control surfaces.

### Layout Paradigm

Use a **radial field with an instrument rail**, not a dashboard grid. The command surface is a large geographic field with asymmetric left navigation and right-side contextual intelligence. Operational pages collapse the field into a precise workbench: prominent search, narrow facets, comparison tables, and decision drawers.

### Signature Elements

1. **Resource filaments:** fine animated route threads with travelling cargo pulses and stateful color.
2. **Orbital readouts:** compact circular and arc-shaped metrics that attach to locations rather than floating as generic KPI cards.
3. **Latitude rules:** faint cartographic lines, coordinate labels, and scientific scale marks that add spatial orientation.

### Interaction Philosophy

Exploration is tactile and location-aware: select a route or region to focus the command field and reveal a relevant intelligence card. Operations are direct: users can search, filter, compare, and request a quote without motion-heavy interruption. AI appears as a contextual briefing or an action recommendation, never as a detached novelty chat bubble.

### Animation

Resource particles move along active routes at a restrained pace to show movement and direction. Risk changes crossfade and pulse only once when state changes. The geographic field subtly drifts on initial load, then holds still for work. Panels enter with 160–240ms opacity and transform transitions; all non-essential motion respects reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System

**Space Grotesk** is the primary UI and data face: geometric, stable, and clear at dense sizes. **DM Mono** supports coordinates, timestamps, prices, and machine-like labels. Headings use Space Grotesk at 600–700 weight with tight tracking; operational labels use mono, uppercase, and spaced letters; body copy remains generous and legible.

### Brand Essence

**ResourceX is the industrial command field for teams that need to see supply exposure early and act with confidence.**

Personality: **clear, capable, vigilant**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are declarative and concise. CTAs are active and decision-oriented. Microcopy explains what changed, why it matters, and what can be done now.

> “One corridor changed. Your contingency is ready.”

> “Compare qualified supply before the window closes.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The logo is a three-path angular resource loop: a compact mark whose intersecting bands suggest a protected route and faceted global terrain. It is paired with a carefully letter-spaced `ResourceX` wordmark, using a small cyan `X` terminal as the identifying detail.

### Signature Brand Color

**Signal Cyan — #38D6EA**

## Style Decisions

- Operational metrics appear as **instrument readouts** with orbital gauge marks, coordinate ticks, and systems-language labels rather than generic KPI cards.
- The ResourceX mark is always treated as a **three-path angular resource loop**, paired with a deliberately letter-spaced wordmark and a cyan `x` terminal.
- **Signal Cyan** is reserved for live flows, focus states, and primary decisions; viridian means verified health, amber means watch state, and coral red means genuine disruption.

## Premium Refinement

The premium direction takes cues from **calm product industrial design**, not from any single interface. Surfaces are quieter and more dimensional, spacing creates a stronger rhythm, and motion is short, restrained, and responsive. The command field remains the visual hero, while all work surfaces feel precise and deliberately constructed.

Opening copy describes the **live network condition or necessary action**, never a generic greeting. Every surface—whether map, route register, or market scan—carries an atlas cue through coordinates, evidence marks, technical linework, or mono system labels.
