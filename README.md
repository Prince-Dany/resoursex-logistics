# resoursex-logistics# 🌍 ResourceX Logistics

### AI-Powered Natural Resource Marketplace & Supply Intelligence Platform

ResourceX Logistics is a **B2B digital marketplace and AI-powered supply intelligence platform** designed to connect natural resource suppliers with industrial buyers while making procurement, pricing, and logistics more efficient.

## 💡 Overview

Industries often struggle with fragmented suppliers, unpredictable prices, limited supply visibility, and inefficient logistics.

**ResourceX Logistics** brings these processes together into a single digital platform where businesses can discover resources, compare suppliers, analyze market intelligence, and manage procurement efficiently.

## 🛢️ Resources Supported

The platform is designed to support resources such as:

* 🪨 Coal
* 🛢️ Crude Oil / Petroleum
* 🔥 Natural Gas
* 🔥 LPG
* ⛽ Diesel
* 🛢️ Furnace Oil
* 🛢️ Petroleum Coke
* 🌾 Biomass
* ♻️ Other industrial resources
